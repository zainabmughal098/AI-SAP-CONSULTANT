## Human review checklist for each candidate

1. Open readable official text for the intended product/edition/release. Check title, section and URL, and resolve mutable selector discrepancies. An indexed extract is not a substitute for this review.
2. Compare every original summary sentence and limited observation with the source. Remove unsupported extensions; retain exclusions and prerequisites. Confirm codes versus app IDs, module/process scope and version differences.
3. Check whether the proposed record is useful enough for its evaluation targets. A transaction-purpose list cannot establish an incident cause, table schema or full procedure. Retain refusal cases where evidence is insufficient.
4. Confirm source usage permissions for the planned short original summary/attribution and later processing. Public availability is not a blanket license; do not copy protected manuals or use gated/internal content. No permission determination has been made here.
5. Check that metadata and text contain no real company records or credentials. Fictional examples must remain in separate DEMO/SIMULATED scenario records.
6. Record actual reviewer identity, review date, disposition and explicit user authorization. Approval must bind to the final exact text/metadata/hash. Do not sign on someone else's behalf or edit generated draft flags to simulate review.

No rejection of a pending candidate means approval. No source ID is proof of authority. Every source remains **pending**, including the directly readable legacy article. The runtime registry and index are unchanged.
